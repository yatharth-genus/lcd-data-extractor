Integration decisions
1. Convert the PaddleOCR detector and recognizer to ONNX and OpenVINO.
2. Integrate OCR alongside the existing icon-detection model.
3. Suppress OCR regions that substantially overlap trusted icon detections.
4. Preserve raw, accepted, and suppressed results during development.
5. Use recognition confidence and domain-format validation for ambiguous regions.
6. Keep the original test split untouched until the complete pipeline is frozen.

Known annotation issue
Recognizer testing revealed incorrect ground truths, including Pu5h and KVA h.
The recognition annotations require an audit before expanded retraining.
